Swiss Alps 2-Day Itinerary

Base: Lucerne • Stoos Ridge & Jungfrau Region

Day 1 Itinerary

Stoos Ridge & Fronalpstock Panorama

Experience the world's steepest funicular, magnificent panoramic ridge walking, and return via a sunset boat ride on Lake Lucerne.

Full Day (~9 hrs) Ridge Walk
Land Route
Lucerne to Ridge
Boat Route
Brunnen to Lucerne
Backup Train
Brunnen Station
08:00 AM Train

Depart Lucerne Main Station

Board the morning train out of Lucerne Main Station toward Schwyz / Stoosbahn.

09:15 AM Stoosbahn Funicular

Arrive at Stoosbahn & World's Steepest Funicular

Ride the futuristic Stoosbahn funicular railway (110% gradient maximum incline) up to the car-free mountain village of Stoos.

09:45 AM – 01:30 PM Ridge Hike & Viewpoint

Fronalpstock Lift & Panoramic Ridge Hike

Take the chairlift up to Fronalpstock. Walk open sections of the ridge path for breathtaking 360° vistas over Lake Lucerne and 10+ surrounding lakes.

01:45 PM Chairlift

Chairlift Down to Stoos Village

Descend back down into Stoos village center via chairlift.

02:30 PM Cable Car & Bus

Morschach Cable Car & Bus to Brunnen Pier

Take the aerial cable car down towards Morschach, then hop on the connecting bus directly to Brunnen Pier.

03:30 PM Buffer & Chocolate

Arrive in Brunnen (Lakefront Stroll)

Safe 1-hour buffer time. Grab a coffee or buy Swiss handmade chocolates at the lakefront shops before boarding.

04:45 PM Lake Steamer Boat

Afternoon Scenic Cruise: Brunnen to Lucerne

Board the final boat from Brunnen back to Lucerne, cruising across Lake Lucerne as the sun sets over the surrounding mountains.

Backup Option (If Boat Missed)

If you miss the 4:45 PM boat, walk ~10 minutes to Brunnen Train Station and board the regular train directly back to Lucerne.

Day 2 Itinerary

Jungfrau Region Grand Loop

Lauterbrunnen waterfalls, Grütschalp cable car, Mürren alpine village, and Gimmelwald farmhouses.

Full Day (~11 hrs) Multi-Transport
Outbound Route Map
Lucerne → Mürren
Return Route Map
Gimmelwald → Lucerne
07:06 AM – 08:54 AM Luzern-Interlaken Express

Lucerne to Interlaken Ost

Scenic express train journey through the Hasli Valley and past Lake Brienz.

09:05 AM – 09:25 AM BOB Line Train

Interlaken Ost to Lauterbrunnen

Local train connection directly into the valley of 72 waterfalls.

Activity • 09:25 AM – 10:50 AM Village Walk

Lauterbrunnen Village & Staubbach Falls

Stroll through the scenic village street, take iconic photos, and view the massive Staubbach Falls cascading down the cliff face.

11:00 AM – 11:04 AM Cable Car (BLM)

Lauterbrunnen to Grütschalp

Aerial cable car ascent offering dramatic cliffside views back over the valley.

11:08 AM – 11:22 AM Cogwheel Train

Grütschalp to Mürren

Scenic narrow-gauge mountain railway along the cliff edge facing Eiger, Mönch & Jungfrau.

Activity • 11:30 AM – 01:30 PM Alpine Terrace Lunch

Explore Mürren & Terrace Lunch

Sit-down lunch on a sun terrace overlooking the high mountain peaks, followed by exploring Mürren village.

01:30 PM – 02:15 PM Downhill Walk

Mürren to Gimmelwald Walk

Easy downhill paved stroll through pastures and traditional Swiss farm scenery.

Activity • 02:15 PM – 03:20 PM Village Sightseeing

Gimmelwald Farm Chalets & Honesty Shop

Explore rustic farm chalets, visit the self-service Honesty Shop for local cheese/crafts, or grab a drink at Mountain Hostel.

03:25 PM – 03:30 PM Schilthornbahn Cable Car

Gimmelwald to Stechelberg

Steep downhill cable car ride descending back to the valley floor.

03:45 PM – 03:58 PM PostBus #141

Stechelberg to Lauterbrunnen

PostBus ride back along the valley floor to Lauterbrunnen station.

Return Journey 04:02 PM – 06:17 PM
